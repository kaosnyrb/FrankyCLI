<LoreFile>
<Summary>
    A once-respected UC Marine has slipped into the cracks of settled space, leaving behind a trail of erratic behavior, scorched outposts, and inexplicable disappearances. Those who’ve crossed paths with them describe a haunted figure, half soldier and half specter of the Colony War’s darkest days. Their movements are unpredictable—sometimes calculated, sometimes violently impulsive. Rumors suggest they’re chasing something only they can see. Bounty offices consider them extremely dangerous, but also a potential window into unresolved war-era secrets.
</Summary>

<StorySeed>
    Archetype Chosen: **“War-scarred veteran turned rogue.”**
    This archetype offers fertile ground for story hooks: fragmented memories, classified operations resurfacing, wartime grudges, and factions eager to bury or exploit the truth. A rogue veteran can cross between lawful and criminal worlds, creating mission chains involving military blacksites, frontier collateral damage, ex-unit members, and psychological unraveling. Their instability and unpredictability ensure each encounter feels both personal and volatile.
</StorySeed>

<TargetProfile>
    Once a decorated UC Marine specializing in combat engineering and breach tactics, the fugitive excelled at improvisational problem-solving under fire. Their unit was disbanded after a disastrous classified operation in the Colony War’s final years. Since then, witnesses report dissociative episodes, hypervigilance, and long stretches of eerily calm focus. They avoid eye contact, mutter unit callouts under their breath, and compulsively inspect structural weaknesses in whatever room they enter. Their break from society began after repeated denials of their request to access sealed war records—records they believe hold answers to what happened to the squad they lost.
</TargetProfile>

<Motives>
    - To uncover the truth of their unit’s final mission, which the UC officially denies ever occurred.  
    - To prevent an imagined—or perhaps real—threat they believe is resurfacing from old war tech.  
    - To prove they are not losing their mind, even as reality around them fractures.  
    - To atone for a perceived failure that their surviving memories cannot fully reconstruct.  
</Motives>

<Rumors>
    - Some spacers claim the Marine carries a stolen UC bioweapon prototype that slowly destabilizes the host.  
    - A Freestar deserter insists the fugitive is actually hunting someone, not fleeing.  
    - A bartender swears they overheard the Marine speaking to an “invisible squadmate.”  
    - Corporate types whisper they were hired for a demolition job that went catastrophically wrong.  
    - Frontier settlers believe the Marine protects isolated homesteads from raiders, then disappears without a word.  
    - One rumor declares they’re already dead and what people see is their “combat ghost.”  
</Rumors>

<Leads>
    - An abandoned military comms relay recently reactivated, broadcasting scrambled distress calls from their old unit.  
    - A black-market medic in a spacer den claims to have treated the Marine’s neurological episodes—under duress.  
    - A razed mining outpost bears explosive signatures matching the fugitive’s breaching charges.  
    - A former squad mate, long retired and in hiding, left behind encrypted journals containing cryptic apologies.  
    - A corporate salvage team uncovered a classified UC device with biometric locks tied to the fugitive’s DNA.  
</Leads>

<Locations>
    - **Redmarsh Firebase Ruins** — remnants of a fortified Colony War site where the Marine’s unit last operated.  
    - **Talon Verge Asteroid Depot** — a derelict UC supply hub turned black-market meeting ground.  
    - **Driftglass Homestead Cluster** — isolated frontier farms the fugitive has been seen silently watching over.  
    - **Nadir-7 Research Cradle** — a half-abandoned lab rumored to house psychological warfare prototypes.  
    - **Shalehook Derelict Frigate** — drifting wreckage carrying encrypted mission logs.  
    - **Unknown Location: “The Stillpoint”** — referenced only in fragmented squad chatter logs.  
    - **Hidden Cache: “Unit Zero Dropbox”** — a clandestine delivery spot used by special ops teams, never mapped.  
</Locations>

<Threats>
    - Unstable war relics and unexploded ordnance scattered across old battlefields.  
    - UC internal security teams who want the fugitive contained before sensitive records surface.  
    - Freelance mercenaries hired by unknown corporate clients to recover “assets” related to the Marine.  
    - The fugitive’s own paranoia: environmental traps, improvised mines, and bouts of violent confusion.  
    - Possible hallucinations that may cause them to misidentify pursuers as wartime enemies.  
</Threats>

<MysteryElements>
    - What truly happened during the Marine’s final mission—and why was every record sealed or erased?  
    - Are the voices and visions haunting the fugitive symptoms of trauma, or echoes of an experimental program?  
    - Is the threat they fear imaginary, or is something from the Colony War genuinely reawakening?  
</MysteryElements>
