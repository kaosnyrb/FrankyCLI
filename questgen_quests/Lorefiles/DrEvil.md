            Across the frontier, hushed conversations tell of a former UC scientist—once a rising star 
            in experimental biotech—now slipping through the Settled Systems under false identities. 

            The UC has issued a discreet yet urgent bounty for their capture, though officials refuse 
            to state what project the scientist was attached to or why their research files were sealed 
            moments after they disappeared.

            The scientist drifts between abandoned med-labs, shuttered research stations, and derelict 
            outposts, leaving behind unauthorized equipment, corrupted data slates, and biohazard warnings 
            hastily scratched out. Each site hints at experiments pushed far beyond legal or ethical limits—
            procedures no oversight body ever approved.

            Every new discovery only deepens the alarm: What breakthrough were they chasing? Which factions 
            benefitted from their research before it spun out of control? And why do survivors claim the 
            scientist speaks about “the next evolution” as if answering to someone—or something—still guiding them?

            This hunt weaves together suppressed research logs, abandoned prototypes, conflicting testimonies, 
            and a trail of subjects changed in ways no one fully understands. Each mission draws investigators 
            deeper into the scientist’s forbidden work—and the terrifying insight that drove them to flee.
        </StorySummary>
