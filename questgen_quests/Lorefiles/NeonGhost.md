        <StorySummary>
            Word spreads through trade lanes and underworld networks of a vanished corporate ghost—
            a former espionage specialist who once worked in Neon's shadows.

            A major megacorporation has quietly posted a bounty for their retrieval, dead or alive.

            The fugitive, known only by whispers as “the Neon Ghost,” moves between criminal
            enclaves, frontier labs, and illegal tech markets. Everywhere they go, systems go dark,
            encrypted vaults mysteriously open, and people who know too much disappear.

            Hunters chasing the Ghost find themselves lost in a maze of failed prototypes,
            corporate sabotage, and whispered cover-ups. Each new lead raises unsettling questions:
            What did the Ghost steal? Who did they betray? And why do multiple corporations seem
            terrified that they were ever involved?

            This pursuit blends espionage, corporate warfare, anonymous identities, and the fear of
            what happens when a tool built for secrecy refuses to be controlled.
        </StorySummary>
