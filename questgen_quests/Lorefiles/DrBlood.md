        <StorySummary>
            Across the Settled Systems, unsettling rumors spread of a former UC biotechnologist—once 
            renowned for breakthroughs in regenerative medicine—now traveling the frontier in secrecy, 
            leaving behind trails of malformed tissue, ruptured growth pods, and subjects who beg not 
            to be examined.

            The UC has quietly issued a high-priority bounty for their capture, but official 
            channels refuse to acknowledge the research project they were assigned to or why most 
            associated files were purged.

            The scientist drifts between abandoned med-labs, derelict ships, and remote frontier 
            settlements, converting each into a grotesque workshop. Hunters find organic lattices 
            fused into ceilings, half-formed organisms in nutrient slurry, and journals written in a 
            mix of clinical precision and religious fervor.

            Each new discovery raises deeper unease: What did the scientist discover that warped their 
            methods beyond ethics? Why do some survivors claim the creations whisper? And why do 
            recovered logs suggest the scientist believes they are being “guided” by something growing 
            within their own altered physiology?

            This pursuit weaves together corrupt research, abandoned prototypes, and the horrifying 
            suggestion that the scientist’s body—and mind—may no longer be entirely their own. Every 
            mission draws hunters closer to the truth behind their final obsession—and what it is 
            becoming.
        </StorySummary>
    </Summary>
