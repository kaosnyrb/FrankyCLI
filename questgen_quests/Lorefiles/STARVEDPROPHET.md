        <StorySummary>
            Something grim stalks the abandoned fringes of the Settled Systems—
            a gaunt figure once known as a deep-space xenocultural analyst,
            now whispered about only as “The Starved Prophet.”

            A sealed directive from a major faction has placed a classified bounty on them:
            no public postings, no explanations.

            Drifting between derelict research stations, remote listening posts,
            and sites of vanished survey teams, the Prophet leaves behind ritual-like
            carvings, scorched data cores, and journals filled with frantic warnings
            about “voices beneath the jump” and “the shape that waits in silence.”

            Hunters who follow the trail report nausea, auditory hallucinations,
            and a sense that the Prophet somehow *knows* they are coming.

            The deeper the pursuit goes, the clearer it becomes:
            the Prophet encountered something in deep space—
            something the faction wants buried,
            and something the Prophet is now desperately trying to share,
            even as their mind disintegrates.

            This pursuit blends paranoia, void-born trauma, derelict horrors,
            and the terrible possibility that the Prophet’s madness
            is not madness at all.
        </StorySummary>
